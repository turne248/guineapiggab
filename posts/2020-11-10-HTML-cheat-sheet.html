My life changed forever on November 4th, 2021, the day I officially became a member of the guinea pig owner community.

Describing this as life-changing may sound like hyperbole, but it’s difficult finding a better descriptor for how someone like myself could go from never owning a pet to, eventually, having as many as 5 (only 5, I say with a wistful sigh) little manure factories and dedicating an entire room of my first house to them, as anything less than transformative.

Of the many obscure hobbies mid-to-late 20’s men like myself choose to dedicate themselves to, guinea pig ownership exists in a league of its own. You will find no performative male Pinterest boards that include cavies; believe me, I have checked. I say this less so to boast, and rather to highlight the dubiousness this whole journey I’ve been on would’ve sounded to me just five years ago.

Despite being asked the question “why guinea pigs?” an innumerable amount over the years, I’ve not spent much time analyzing or articulating what owning these critters means to me. Only after experiencing several losses within my guinea pig family over the course of 2026 did I find myself reflecting deeply on this chapter of my life and how I am to make sense of it. Over time I had built up a complex whereby I believed that prioritizing this niche pet interest would be reflexively alienating. However, upon further investigation, I’ve come to recognize that I’ve never really tried to get others to understand it.
